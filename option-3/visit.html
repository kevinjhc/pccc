I Getting here

By foot, transit, bike, or car

Bus

SEPTA routes

Several SEPTA routes run nearby along Front Street, 2nd Street, and Columbus Boulevard.

Subway

Market-Frankford Line

Market-Frankford Line to 2nd Street Station, then walk south about 15 to 20 minutes.

Bike

Indego Bike Share

Indego Bike Share stations are nearby in Queen Village and Society Hill.

Parking

Street parking

Limited street parking on nearby blocks. Please respect residential permit zones.

II Plaza map

What you'll find

  1. 1
    Statue of Peace

    A bronze girl and an empty chair. You are welcome to sit beside her.

  2. 2
    Inscription

    An etched metal sign tells the history behind the memorial.

  3. 3
    Benches

    Concrete and wood benches with low lighting.

  4. 4
    Native gardens

    Witch hazel, magnolia, grasses, phlox, and spring tulips.

  5. 5
    Lighting

    Pedestrian lights keep the plaza welcoming after dark.

  6. 6
    Street trees

    Black gum trees that turn red in the fall.

IV While you're here

A memorial and a neighborhood garden

  1. Sit with her. You may sit in the empty chair beside the statue. Many visitors do.

  2. Flowers are welcome. Please keep tributes off the planting beds.

  3. Keep it quiet. Neighbors live across the street.

  4. Dogs on leash and out of the gardens.
