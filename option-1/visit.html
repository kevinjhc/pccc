Getting here

  • BusSeveral SEPTA routes run nearby along Front Street, 2nd Street, and Columbus Boulevard.
  • SubwayMarket-Frankford Line to 2nd Street Station, then walk south about 15 to 20 minutes.
  • BikeIndego Bike Share stations are nearby in Queen Village and Society Hill.
  • ParkingLimited street parking on nearby blocks. Please respect residential permit zones.

While you're here

The Plaza is a memorial and a neighborhood garden. Please help keep it peaceful.

  • Sit with herYou may sit in the empty chair beside the statue. Many visitors do.
  • FlowersFlowers and small tributes are welcome. Please keep them off the planting beds.
  • NeighborsKeep voices and music low. People live across the street.
  • DogsKeep dogs leashed and out of the gardens.
  • GroupsPlanning a school trip, group visit, or vigil? Let us know.
